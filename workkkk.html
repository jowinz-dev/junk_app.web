Our Story

A plastic bottle thrown in a trench. Scrap metal piled in a backyard.
Polythene bags clogging drainage channels
In Kampala, small waste creates a big problem -
blocked drains, dirty streets and unhealthy neighbourhoods.
Junk App was built to make cleaning simple. When a home posts its junk,
a local collector picks it up systematically.
Your small action keeps your home clean and your community breathing.
