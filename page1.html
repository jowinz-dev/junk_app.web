Our Partners

At Junk App, we know that solving urban waste management takes more than just smart technology—it takes strong collaboration. By partnering with certified recyclers, civic authorities, and digital network providers, we seamlessly connect every step of the waste-to-value supply chain to keep our communities clean, sustainable, and powered by opportunity.

Badge: Payout Integration

Powers real-time Mobile Money disbursements for independent community waste collectors and household scrap sellers directly and securely through the Junk App dispatch ecosystem.

Badge: E-waste Handler

Handles the safe extraction, dismantling, and eco-compliant recycling of dead electronics, batteries, and discarded household appliances booked seamlessly through our mobile platform.

Badge: Plastic Recycler

Together with Eco Brixs, we divert post-consumer plastic from local dumpsites and channel it into manufacturing high-quality, eco-friendly paving bricks and sustainable building materials.

Badge: Civic Partner

Working hand-in-hand with civic councils, we combine Junk App's smart route optimization with municipal frameworks to make neighborhood waste collection faster, cleaner, and fully compliant.

Let’s collaborate to build efficient, zero-waste supply chains that power cleaner communities and create sustainable economic opportunities together.
